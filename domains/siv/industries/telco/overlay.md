# Telco addendum — SIV

## Additional concepts

- **MobileServiceInstance** — A live voice, messaging, or data service delivered over a mobile network.
- **NetworkServiceComponent** — A mobile-network capability forming part of a delivered service.

## Additional relationships

- MobileServiceInstance deliveredToSubscriber Subscriber (many-to-one)

## Additional roles

- **Mobile Service Designer** — Person role that maps MVNO products to host-network service capabilities. Bearer: person. Permissions: ServiceSpecification:read, ServiceSpecification:write, ProductServiceMapping:read, ProductServiceMapping:write.

## Regulatory notes

- Identifier, subscriber, usage, and service associations retain effective dates and source-system provenance where required for audit.
- Personally identifiable and communications data are accessed according to applicable privacy, retention, and lawful-process controls.
