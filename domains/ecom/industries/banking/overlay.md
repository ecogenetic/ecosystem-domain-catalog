# Banking addendum — E-Commerce

A bank's digital storefront sells financial products and value-added services rather than goods:
loan products (personal loans, overdrafts, credit cards) that fulfil by disbursement after
affordability and KYC gates, insurance add-ons, and prepaid value-added services (VAS) sold through
the banking channel — electricity tokens, airtime and data top-ups, vouchers — that provision
instantly from the customer's account. Nothing ships: fulfilment is disbursement, policy issue,
or token delivery.

## Additional concepts

- **LoanProduct** — a Product representing a credit offering (personal loan, overdraft, credit card) with an amount range, term range, and interest rate; buying one is applying for it.
- **LoanApplication** — an Order specialised for credit: it carries the requested amount and term and moves through affordability assessment to approval and disbursement.
- **AffordabilityCheck** — the mandated assessment of income, expenses, and credit record that gates every loan approval.
- **Disbursement** — a Fulfilment that pays an approved loan amount into the customer's account; the "delivery" of a loan.
- **ValueAddedService** — a Product for a prepaid third-party service sold through the banking channel: electricity tokens, airtime, data, vouchers, bill payments.
- **ElectricityToken** — the prepaid utility token issued for an electricity VAS purchase, keyed to the customer's meter number.
- **InsuranceAddOn** — a Product attaching optional cover (credit life, device, funeral) to a loan or standalone purchase.

## Additional relationships

- LoanApplication appliesForProduct LoanProduct (many-to-one); the application is the order, the loan product is the catalog item.
- AffordabilityCheck assessesApplication LoanApplication (one-to-one); approval is blocked until the check passes — a regulatory requirement, not a business preference.
- Disbursement paysOutApplication LoanApplication (one-to-one); disbursement only follows an approved application with a passed affordability check.
- ElectricityToken issuedForOrder Order (one-to-one); the token is generated by the utility and delivered digitally with the receipt.
- InsuranceAddOn attachedToApplication LoanApplication (many-to-one); optional cover priced into the repayment.
- VAS purchases reuse the base flow: cart → order → payment from account → instant digital fulfilment (token or top-up), no shipping.

## Additional roles

- **LoanOfficerRole** (bearer: person) — reviews applications the automated affordability check refers for manual decision; permissions: LoanApplication:read, LoanApplication:write, AffordabilityCheck:read, LoanProduct:read, Disbursement:read
- **VASOperationsRole** (bearer: person) — monitors VAS provider integrations and re-issues failed tokens or top-ups; permissions: ValueAddedService:read, ElectricityToken:read, ElectricityToken:write, Order:read, Payment:read

## Regulatory notes

- Responsible-lending law (e.g. NCA affordability assessment) makes the AffordabilityCheck mandatory before any credit approval; generated workflows must gate approval on a passed check and record the assessment for audit.
- KYC must be verified before loan disbursement and above thresholds for VAS purchases; AML monitoring applies to disbursements.
- Interest rates, fees, and total cost of credit must be disclosed on the LoanProduct before application — price transparency is regulated, not optional.
- Declined applications require an adverse-action reason recorded and communicated to the customer.
- VAS token issuance is transactional: a debited account with no delivered token must auto-reverse or re-issue — never leave the customer debited without value.
